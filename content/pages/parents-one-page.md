## Start a conversation

- "That ad said 'risk-free bet.' What do you think that means?"
- "Does that game have loot boxes? Do you know your odds?"
- "Do your friends bet on games? What do you make of it?"

Pick an ordinary moment. Be curious, not alarmed. Listen more than you talk.

## Explain how it works

1. **The odds favor the company.** Bettors as a group lose over time.
2. **Chance has no memory.** A win is never "due."
3. **"Free" bets have strings.** You deposit and bet first.

## Keep access out of reach

- Don't place bets or buy scratch tickets for your child.
- Turn on purchase approval: Screen Time, Family Link, console family settings.
- Watch for gambling charges, gift cards and payment apps.

## If your child already bets

- **Stay calm.** "I'm glad you told me."
- **Get the details.** What, with whom, whose money, how they feel.
- **Skip the lecture.** Threats end the conversation.
- **Watch money and mood.** Debts, hiding losses or low mood mean get help.
- **A debt with pressure or threats** is a safety issue. Involve the school or police.

## Get help

<p class="onepage-line"><strong>988</strong> Call or text in a crisis, 24/7</p>
