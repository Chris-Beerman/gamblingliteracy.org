## Who this is for

Middle and high school teachers, especially in health, math, PE and advisory. School counselors, social workers and psychologists. Coaches, athletic directors and youth workers.

Parents and caregivers are welcome here too, and have [their own page](/parents/).

## Two things to keep in mind

::: research
School gambling programs reliably improve what students know and fix common misconceptions. Whether they reduce gambling or harm later is much less certain. Few studies have followed students long enough to find out. See [what the evidence says](/research-and-advocacy/#what-programs-change).
:::

::: practical
So treat education as one part of prevention, not all of it. What adults do matters too: noticing, staying connected, limiting easy access, questioning the ads, and knowing where to send someone who needs help.
:::

## What this guide is not

It is not a curriculum, a screening tool or clinical advice. It doesn't ask you to label any student "at risk." It helps you teach every young person well and notice when one of them needs more.
